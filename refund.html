1. Overview

At JusClosers (operated by Estate Hive, Bengaluru, India), we want you to be confident in your purchase. This Refund Policy outlines the terms under which refunds are available for our various offerings.

Before the session: A full refund is available if requested at least 24 hours before the scheduled masterclass session.

3. Training Programs & Other Paid Services

7-Day Satisfaction Policy: If you are not satisfied with a training program, you may request a refund within 7 days of your purchase date.

  • All approved refunds will be credited to the original payment method used at the time of purchase.

Refunds will not be provided in the following cases:
