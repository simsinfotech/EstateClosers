Questions

Before youapply.

Is the audit really free?
Yes. The mystery calls, the leak report and the walkthrough are free, with no obligation. The lead revival pilot is optional and mostly paid on results.
Will your mystery calls disturb my team?
No. They're normal enquiries, the same as any buyer would make. We don't waste your team's time beyond a typical first call, and we tell you exactly which enquiries were ours in the report.
How does the lead revival pilot get priced?
A small setup fee plus commission on deals that close from the leads you share. Exact terms depend on your deal size and are agreed before we start.
Is our data kept confidential?
Yes. We sign an NDA on request, use your recordings and leads only for your audit or pilot, and delete them afterwards if you ask us to.
How long does it take?
Your leak report is ready within 7 working days of receiving your recordings and numbers. The pilot runs for 21 days.
How many audits do you take?
A limited number each month, because our closing team does every review by hand. If we're full, we'll tell you the next available slot.
