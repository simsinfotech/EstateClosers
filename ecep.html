Be honest with yourself for 60 seconds

  • Sunday evening arrives and something in your chest tightens. You've felt it for years. You've just stopped mentioning it.

The questions in your head right now

Answered straight.

Do I need to quit my job?

No. Training runs on evenings and weekends. Field phase needs weekend availability plus some weekday flexibility. Most founding students transition gradually, and the program is built exactly for that. You risk your evenings, not your salary.

Zero real estate experience. Can I do this?

The program assumes zero background. What it demands: you can talk to people, take feedback without sulking, and show up when it's hard. Skill we build. Spine you bring.

Where do leads come from?

From us. We spend the marketing money; leads are assigned to you in field phase. Your job is closing, not lead generation. Deliberate. Permanent.

What exactly do I earn per deal?

The full structure, slabs and bonuses are shown on your enrollment call, before you pay, and put in writing. We don't publish it on a webpage. We do stand behind it on paper.

I'm not in Bangalore.

Batch 1 is Bangalore-only; the field phase runs on Bangalore inventory. Hyderabad, Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi open in the coming months. Apply and pick your city. Founding access flows to the waitlist first.

Why only 20 seats?

Because every student gets real leads and shadowed site visits, and that doesn't scale like a YouTube video. Twenty is what we can coach properly. The cap is the product.
