Three Prestige launches are coming to North Bengaluru. This channel partner wants to talk you out of two of them.

Pre-launch pricing typically runs 8 to 15 percent under launch. The token is fully refundable. And the firm whose phone rings for all three says the first job is telling each buyer which launch is not for them.

Somewhere between the Hebbal flyover and the airport, three Prestige projects are about to open their pre-launch windows within weeks of each other. A township on the Satellite Town Ring Road with homes starting around ₹70 lakh. A set of 2, 3 and 3.5 BHK homes on Thanisandra Main Road between ₹1.7 crore and ₹2.7 crore. And a small run of 4,000 to 4,800 sq ft townvillas near the airport corridor, priced from ₹10 crore to ₹12.5 crore.*

By the time you read this, several hundred channel partners will be pushing the same developer renders into the same audiences with the same two words: Book Now. One Bengaluru firm has decided to do the opposite.

Estate Hive, a RERA-registered channel partner that worked the corridor's last four big launches, has built its business on a line most brokers would consider commercial suicide. "We tell you which launch not to buy," says the firm's founder. "If it is the wrong project for you, we would rather lose the deal in the first five minutes than after you have paid a token."

The window most buyers only hear about afterwards

Every big launch in this city has a quieter phase before it. The developer opens an Expression of Interest, or EOI, window. Buyers register, pay a token, and are placed in a queue. Weeks later, when the project's RERA registration is issued, the people in that queue are converted first. Everyone else meets the project on launch day, at launch pricing, competing for what is left.

Three things make that window worth understanding, and none of them is a sales trick.

The price. Pre-launch pricing typically runs 8 to 15 percent under expected launch pricing. On a ₹70 lakh home that is a gap of ₹5,60,000 to ₹10,50,000. On a ₹2.7 crore home it is ₹21,60,000 to ₹40,50,000. The table below shows the arithmetic for each ticket.

The order. Allocation preference follows EOI order. The corner units, the high floors and the good facing go to the people who registered first. This is not manufactured urgency. It is how the queue works.

The exit. The token is 100 percent refundable if you decide not to proceed. "Reserve your priority. Pay nothing you can't get back," is how Estate Hive puts it, and the refund clause is written on the receipt.

What an EOI is not. It is not a booking, not an allotment and not a sale. Until the project's RERA registration is issued, no unit is confirmed and no possession date can be promised. What an EOI buys is your place in the queue and the pre-launch price attached to it.

The analyst who says no

Estate Hive's method is closer to a research desk than a sales floor. Before the firm will take a token from anyone, it writes what it calls a verdict note on the launch: a fair-price estimate against the expected launch price, resale comparables from the corridor's recent launches, the configuration it would buy and the one it would avoid, and a line every note carries near the top: who this is not for.

"Most of what a buyer hears in a pre-launch is a render and a countdown," says the firm's founder. "What they actually need is the number the flat will trade at in three years, and a straight answer on whether they should be in the queue at all."

The second thing the firm does differently is the one buyers notice first. How the window closes

An EOI window usually runs three to six weeks. The order in which it fills matters more than the day it closes, because allocation follows the queue.

Seven questions to ask before any EOI

Estate Hive's fit check ends with the firm's own checklist, which it says most buyers have never been handed by a broker. The desk reproduces it here.

  1. Is RERA registration in process, and when is the number expected? Ask for it in writing the day it is issued.
  2. What exactly is the token, and is the refund clause on the receipt? A verbal promise is not a clause.
  3. What is the pre-launch price, and what is the developer's expected launch price? The gap between the two is the reason you are in the queue.
  4. How is allocation ordered, and where am I in it? Priority is only worth something if you know your position.
  5. Carpet area or super built-up? Which number is the price quoted on, and what does it do to the rate per square foot?
  6. What is the payment schedule after allotment? Map it to your EMI before you register, not after.
  7. Who am I dealing with? The developer or a channel partner, and is that partner RERA-registered? Estate Hive's number is at the bottom of this page.

Questions readers are asking

Is an EOI a booking?

No. An Expression of Interest is a priority registration. It places you in the allocation queue at pre-launch pricing. No unit is confirmed and nothing is sold until the project's RERA registration is issued and a formal booking follows.

Is the token really refundable?

Yes. If you decide not to proceed, the token is refunded in full under the developer's EOI terms. Estate Hive asks for the refund clause to be written on the receipt, and will not take a token where it is not.

Can I do this from outside India?

Yes. NRI buyers get a video walkthrough, a digital EOI process and end-to-end management of the paperwork. The STRR township, in particular, was designed with this buyer in mind.

Can I see the project before I register?

Thanisandra runs preview lounge slots on weekends. The townvillas are shown by private appointment only. For the STRR township, the analyst call covers the site, the corridor and the township plan before any token is discussed.

Will I get calls from seventeen brokers?

Not from this enquiry. One enquiry, one analyst call. Your number is never shared with any other channel partner or agency.

Are the prices on this page final?

No. All pricing is indicative pre-launch pricing and subject to change. Exact pricing for unregistered inventory cannot be quoted. The analyst call gives you the current pre-launch number for the configuration you want.
