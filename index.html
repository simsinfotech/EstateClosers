Questions

Askedand answered.

I've never sold anything. Can I really do this?
Yes. The Accelerator starts from zero. You don't need experience, a sales degree or contacts. If you can hold a conversation and you're willing to practise, you can learn to close.
Where does the training happen?
The Accelerator's lessons are online, and its field training happens in Bengaluru, where you'll call our leads and meet buyers at real sites. Ask us about the 1% Realtor Club and Private programs.
Do you give leads?
In the Accelerator, yes. From day 31 you dial real leads from our brokerage, so you don't spend on ads. In the 1% Realtor Club, you learn to generate your own leads with ads and content.
How much do the programs cost?
Fees depend on the program. Send an enquiry and our team will walk you through them on a call.
Is the 90-day refund real?
Yes. Complete the Accelerator, every lesson, every session and every assigned call, and if you don't close a deal within 90 days, we refund the full fee. Full terms are shared in writing before you join.
What is the Certified Remote Closer program?
A pre-recorded course on closing sales over the phone, open worldwide. You finish with a written exam and a scored mock call. Pass both and you get a certificate with a unique ID that companies can verify, plus career assistance.
How does hiring through JusClosers work for companies?
Share your brief and we send a shortlist of at least 3 certified closers within 21 days. You interview them on a real call and hire who you like. If a hire leaves within 60 days, we replace them once, free. Fees are shared on a call.
Do you train corporate sales teams?
Yes. Our trainer trains your employees on-site or live online, using your product, your leads and your real call recordings, and measures call scores before and after. For large teams, we can also train and certify your in-house trainers to run the method themselves.
Do you only train for real estate?
No. Real estate is where we started, because we sell there every week. Today we train closers for real estate, financial services, education and SaaS and B2B tech. The closing method is the same; the examples and role-plays change for each industry.
Will you place me in a job after training?
We don't promise jobs. Certified closers can join our talent pool for free, companies hire from it, and strong performers get introduced to them. You never pay us to be placed. Your results decide your opportunities.
We're a company. Can we hire from you or train our team?
Yes. You can hire pre-trained closers from our pool, have us train your existing sales team, or have us deploy a closer on your leads. Send an enquiry as a company and we'll call you.
Which program should I start with?
New to real estate? The Accelerator. Already selling and want to grow? The 1% Realtor Club. Not sure? Send an enquiry and we'll tell you honestly which one fits.
