Everyone teachesleads. Nobodyteaches the close.

Getting a buyer's number is the easy part. The moment between the first hello and the booking is where almost everyone gets stuck.

01 · THE CALL

"Hello?"

The phone rings and your mind goes blank. No first line, so the buyer hangs up in ten seconds.

02 · THE BROCHURE

"Just send me the details."

You send it. They go quiet. You send "any update?" three times. They never reply.

03 · THE FAMILY

"Let me ask my family."

The visit went well. Then the questions came at home, you weren't there, and the deal died at the dinner table.
